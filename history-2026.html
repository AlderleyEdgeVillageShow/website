The Review

The 82nd Alderley Edge Village Show took place this year at a new venue in St Philip and St James Church where we welcomed 75 Exhibitors who collectively showed just over 350 entries in a wonderful showcase of talent and creativity.

In the face of challenging summer weather with a prolonged dry spell and extreme heat, our talented growers and makers lovingly prepared fabulous floral displays, giant vegetables, perfect fruits, delicious cakes, bakes and preserves, all sitting alongside a stunning collection of art and crafts.

Thanks to the efforts and commitment not only of our Exhibitors but also our hardworking Volunteers, Café Team and our dedicated Judges, visitors were able to enjoy so many marvellous sights whilst catching up with friends over a hot drink and cake at our Pop-Up Café.

Without every single one of you we would not have a show, we cannot thank you enough for your participation and support. Special thanks also to the local businesses that helped – something we do not take for granted and greatly appreciate, your support is invaluable in keeping this essential community event alive. Charitable Efforts

Every year a key aim of the show is to raise funds for charity. Our chosen charity for 2026 is The North West Air Ambulance. Thanks to your incredible generosity, we are delighted to confirm a donation of £1000 to help power their next lifesaving mission, enabling their highly skilled crew of critical care Paramedics and Doctors to provide pre-hospital care and treatment to the most critically ill and unwell people in our community.
